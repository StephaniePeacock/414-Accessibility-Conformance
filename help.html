Frequently Asked Questions

About Classes
Do I need previous pottery experience to take a class?
Not at all! Our classes are designed for all skill levels, including complete beginners. Our instructors will guide you through each step, from preparing the clay to creating your finished piece.
What should I wear to a pottery class?
Wear comfortable clothing that you don't mind getting a little messy. Clay washes out of most fabrics, but we recommend avoiding long, loose sleeves and wearing closed-toe shoes.
Can children participate in pottery classes?
Yes! We offer family-friendly classes for children ages 8 and older. Participants under 16 must be accompanied by a registered adult.
Do I need to bring my own supplies?
No! We provide everything you need, including clay, tools, pottery wheels, and glazing materials. Just bring yourself and a willingness to get your hands dirty!
Your Creations
How long does it take to complete a pottery piece?
Most pieces can be shaped during a single class. However, pottery requires several stages of drying, firing, and glazing before it's ready to take home. The entire process typically takes 2–4 weeks.
When can I pick up my finished pottery?
We'll notify you by email when your pieces are ready for pickup. Finished pottery is generally available within 2–4 weeks, depending on drying and kiln schedules.
Can I bring home the pottery I make during class?
Absolutely! Every piece you create is yours to keep. Once your pottery has been fired and glazed, we'll let you know when it's ready to be collected.
What happens if my pottery cracks or breaks during firing?
Pottery is a natural and sometimes unpredictable process. Although we take every precaution, cracking, warping, and other imperfections can occasionally occur. If a piece is damaged during firing, we'll work with you to discuss your options.
Private Events
Do you offer private group classes or parties?
Yes! We offer private pottery sessions for birthdays, team-building events, celebrations, and other special occasions. Contact us via our contact form to discuss group sizes and scheduling.
What is your cancellation policy?
We understand that plans change! Classes can be rescheduled or canceled up to 48 hours before the scheduled session. Cancellations made within 48 hours are non-refundable.
Studio Details
Do you sell pottery made in the studio?
Yes! We have a selection of handcrafted pottery available for purchase in our studio. Each piece is individually made and glazed, making every item one of a kind.
Can I use the studio without enrolling in a class?
We offer open-studio sessions for experienced potters who would like access to our equipment and workspace. Contact us for availability, membership options, and studio requirements.
Do you offer gift certificates?
Yes! Gift certificates are available for purchase and can be used toward pottery classes, workshops, and select studio experiences. They're a great way to give someone a creative experience!
Where are you located?
We are located at 1670 Devore Road in San Bernardino, CA 92407 - just off the 215 North, Exit 54A.
Is parking available at the studio?
Yes! Complimentary parking is available near the studio. We recommend arriving about 10 minutes before your scheduled class so you have time to get settled.
