About Us

Mudslinger's Pottery Studio is a community of artists and enthusiasts who are passionate about pottery. We believe that creativity is something to be shared, explored, and celebrated. Located in the foothills of the San Bernardino Mountains, our studio is a welcoming space where clay, imagination, and community come together. Whether you're picking up pottery for the first time or refining skills you've practiced for years, there's a place for you at our tables. We offer weekly classes for all experience levels, providing hands-on instruction in a relaxed, encouraging environment. From learning the basics of shaping and glazing to more advanced techniques, our goal is to help everyone discover the joy of creating something uniquely their own.

We're passionate about bringing people together through art, creativity, and shared experiences. Beyond our classes, we host free monthly community events invite neighbors of all ages to get their hands dirty, try something new, and connect with others.

Looking for something interesting and unique for your next party or team building meeting? Rent out studio space for a private event, offering a memorable and creative setting for celebrations, gatherings, and special occasions. Whether you're joining us for a class, attending a community event, or simply stopping by to see what's happening, you'll find a studio built around inspiration, connection, and the simple pleasure of playing with dirt.

Mudslingers Pottery Studio began in 2008 as a small, independent pottery workshop founded by longtime ceramic artist Margaret "Maggie" Sullivan. What started as a modest rented workspace with a handful of pottery wheels and a few shelves of handmade pieces quickly attracted a community of people eager to learn the craft. Maggie had always believed that pottery was about more than shaping clay; it was about slowing down, experimenting, and finding joy in creating something with your own hands. As word spread, the little workshop grew into a welcoming creative space where beginners and experienced artists alike could share ideas, learn new techniques, and discover their own artistic voices.

In 2014, Mudslingers moved into its current San Bernardino location, expanding its classroom space and opening its doors to a wider community. Over the years, the studio introduced weekly classes, free monthly community gatherings, and private pottery experiences, making the art of ceramics accessible to even more people. While the studio has grown and evolved, its original philosophy remains unchanged: everyone has a little creativity waiting to be uncovered. Today, Mudslingers continues to honor its handmade roots, bringing together local artists, students, and pottery enthusiasts through a shared love of clay, craftsmanship, and community.
